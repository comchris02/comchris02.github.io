STORY

How did you first meet?

A conference room with the scent of moist Jimmy Johns wafting through the air - Shelby and Chris had a chance meeting. Only Chris was smart enough to notice that they were actually meeting.

While that was THE first meeting, there were a few missed opportunities:

  • When Shelby and Chris lived in Las Vegas, their homes were 15 minutes apart
  • Shelby studied Entertainment Engineering at UNLV and occasionally visited FTSI, where Chris worked
  • Both worked on Pandora: The World of Avatar
  • When they finally connected on Facebook, they had 36 friends in common

This is actually Shelby and Chris' first photo together, many years before the first meeting:

2018 Thea Awards

Now, with a strong geographical hurdle (Chris in Long Beach, CA and Shelby in Orlando, FL), Chris started to lay the groundwork.

Wait, with that distance — how did it all come together?

Chris made the first move, and thank goodness — Shelby is clueless!

What was your most memorable date?

Long distance meant our dates were often full-blown adventures:

  • 1st date - Orlando. SAK Improv Club (Home of Wayne Brady)
  • 3rd date — Orlando. Chris pointing out his name in the Eras Tour credits
  • 4th date — LA. Chris getting a glimpse of just how deeply Shelby shows up for her friends
  • 5th date — Japan. Overlapping Trips to see the fall colors
  • We lost count by this point - Club 33 dinner for Shelby’s 33rd

The most spontaneous thing we’ve done

Chris proposed — before we lived in the same state — in a haunted house, followed by a surprise party with friends and family from around the world.

You live in the same state now... right?

We do! Chris has relocated to Orlando, Florida and we are settling into our new home together.
