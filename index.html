The red rope reveal

This was never going to be an ordinary walk down the aisle.

What began as a familiar hotel ceremony turned into a 215-person adventure from Cabana Bay to a closed water park—and ended exactly where the red thread had always been leading.

1

The ceremony that wasn’t

Guests gathered on the sand at Cabana Bay, with Krakatau rising beyond the arbor. Zach Honea began the ceremony and traced the invisible thread through Shelby and Chris’s near-misses: Las Vegas, FTSI, Pandora, and even the same dance floor five years before they met.

“What’s meant to be will be.”

2

The rope wouldn’t end

When Zach reached for the end of the ceremonial rope, more rope kept coming—from beneath the sand. Julia Wessels arrived with the essential realization: if this wasn’t the end of Shelby and Chris’s journey, this couldn’t be the place for their wedding.

“What if we follow the rope?”

3

All 215 guests went with it

Children led the way. The red rope looped through plantings, wrapped around pathway lights, and took a few Super Mario–style warp pipes whenever it needed to get beneath a walkway. It crossed the security gate and drew everyone toward Volcano Bay. On the other side, Fate and Destiny cocktails were waiting.

A magnetometer, a closed water park, and a few feet of sand…

4

Chris gave his vows an encore

At the rope’s true end, Shelby and Chris exchanged rings they had handmade for each other. When Chris began his vows, the microphone wasn’t working—so he delivered them privately to Shelby. Then she did what she always does: lifted up someone she loves and encouraged him to be his best. With a working microphone, Chris shared his vows again so everyone could hear them.

Two deliveries. One very enthusiastic “I do.”

5

Dinner, cake, and DANCING IN THE WATER!!!

Once the vows were official, the adventure became a party. Everyone gathered for dinner, Shelby and Chris cut the cake beneath Krakatau, and then all those wonderfully committed good sports took the celebration straight into the water—and danced.

Because a dry dance floor would have been much too ordinary.

To everyone who followed the rope

Thank you for playing along.

We considered crazy restaurants, historic venues, cruises, and even talked to three different reality TV producers. But as our guest list grew, we realized what mattered most was bringing our favorite people together and making the wedding genuinely fun for everyone.

You traveled, dressed tropical-funky-formal, experienced a pre-show at a wedding, watched everything go spectacularly wrong, followed the rope, crossed security, and committed to every ridiculous turn. All 215 of you were wonderful sports, and the joy you brought to the adventure made the night better than we ever imagined.

To Jordan, thank you for the show writing that gave the adventure its voice. To Ashley, our queen of logistics and macramé, thank you for holding every detail together. And to our Keepers of the Cord, Charlotte and the Universal teams, our wedding party, our parents, and our families: thank you for believing in an unconventional idea and making it real.

Chris may be an engineer accustomed to believing 1 + 1 = 2. Shelby keeps proving that together, 1 + 1 can become something so much greater.

With all our love and gratitude, Shelby & Chris
