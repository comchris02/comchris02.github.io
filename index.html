STORY

Two nerds walked into a room, and spent the next two years circling the globe to be together.

How did you first meet?

Scene: A conference room with moist Jimmy Johns wafting through the air - Shelby and Chris had a chance meeting. Only Chris was smart enough to notice that they were actually meeting.

While that was THE first meeting, there were a few missed opportunities:

  • When Shelby and Chris lived in Las Vegas, their homes were 15 minutes apart
  • Shelby studied Entertainment Engineering at UNLV and occasionally visited FTSI, where Chris worked
  • Both worked on Pandora the World of Avatar
  • When they finally connected on Facebook, they had 36 friends in common

This is actually Shelby and Chris's first photo together, many years before the first meeting:

2018 Thea Awards

Now, with a strong geographical hurdle (Chris in Long Beach, CA and Shelby in Orlando, FL), Chris started to lay the groundwork.

Wait, with that distance — how did it all come together?

Chris made the first move, and thank goodness — Shelby is clueless!
